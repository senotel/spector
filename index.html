Why did you skip?

Vision Spector

Watch the way you normally watch Shorts. Press Next (or swipe up on the bar, or the Down arrow) the moment you would swipe away. Let a video loop if you would rewatch it.

Press Like, Comment, Share or Repost whenever you would on your real feed. They do nothing except note that you would have. Comment lets you type what you would say, if you want.

After every video you skip before the end, it asks why. Tap a reason, write your own, or just keep scrolling.

This page is Senotel research. It records which videos you watch, for how long, what you like and why you swipe, to train Senotel's AI to understand what people enjoy. No account, no name unless you add one, nothing else about you. For ages 13 and up.
